# Task Eggspeditions 3.0 Rebuild
## Post-Prestige Planetary Challenges
### Base Overview
- 3 Tiered Challenge Structure -> 3 Tiers of Bonus Reward, 3 Tiers of Goal Leveling
- Costs Soul Eggs to Discover a Planet -> Increases for each planet discovered
- You will start with a base amount of material after beating tier 1, either common research tiers or money
### Arcturus - Energy
#### Main Gimmick: Dark Energy Eggs are used to Fuel Production Equipment to Make Money
- Money is not directly produced from the eggs but is make through "burning" eggs into power to make money
### Ravnar - Time
#### Main Gimmick: Temporal Flux Buildup
- The more time eggs you produce, the more temporal flux is produced
- Temporal Flux, decreases egg production & chicken gain down to 0 as it approaches its capacity
- Can be vented a few times in a run using different methods (undecided)
### Xylok - Peace
#### Main Gimmick: Prices are low for Peace Eggs
- Slower pace, like enlightenment eggs
- Chickens are produced by choosing to hatch eggs, rather than internal hatcheries or the click to hatch button
### Triton - Darkness
#### Main Gimmick: Darkness balancing
- Abyssal eggs must be used to balance out light output, too much light and the run collapses (abyssal chickens die in the light, you lose some based on your light level but ), too little and the abyssal egg value drops like a rock, just right and you keep production stable and egg value stable
### Hereth - Lava
#### Main Gimmick: Market Manipulation
- Lava eggs are used to make different raw materials, you then purchase stock by selling lava eggs for money, then manipulate a fake stock market by oversaturating or desaturating different resource based indices
- Bottom out the market and it will result in monetary devaluation
### Malak - Light
#### Main Gimmick: Light Source Production
- Scale up your light's sphere of influence by feeding the "orb" your light eggs, they can be sold for money or fed to increase their value, The orb's egg cost scales, and may decay overtime
## Post-Ascension Harvester Challenges
### Base Overview
- Acts like a Tier 4-6 Challenge, Eggspeditions are beaten, and a harvester is built (Tier 1), progress on that planet is no longer lost on Ascension, so for a tier 1 harvester, the first tier of challenge is preserved, for tier 3 all of the challenge tiers are preserved
- Standard goals are replaced by a funding goal, feeding eggs, chickens and money into building out the harvester on top of the gimmick for the planet.
